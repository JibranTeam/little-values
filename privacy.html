Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

What we collect

When you join our list through the signup form on this site, we collect the email address you enter. The form is handled by Formspree and the address is sent to us at thejimmylab@gmail.com. We use it only to send free activities and news about the book.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share your email address with anyone. We do not create accounts on this site, and we do not take payments here.

Site visits

We use Vercel Analytics to see anonymous page views, so we know which pages are read. It does not identify individual visitors.

Children

This site is for parents, teachers and guardians. Children should not enter personal information here. Please join the list as a grown-up.

Your choices

To stop receiving our emails, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to thejimmylab@gmail.com and ask to be removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: thejimmylab@gmail.com.
